# Morgan Stanley Internal Memorandum: Strategic Reinvestment & Workforce Management Action
**Date:** March 18, 2026  
**From:** Office of the Chief Operating Officer & Human Resources  
**To:** Managing Directors, Business Unit Heads, and Regional Operating Officers  
**Subject:** Operational Efficiency Initiatives, Pre-Tax Severance Charges, and Headcount Targets  

---

## 1. Context and Strategic Rationale
During the first quarter of 2026, Morgan Stanley initiated a global workforce management action designed to flatten management hierarchies, streamline operational overhead, and create expense capacity for strategic reinvestment into core institutional and digital wealth platforms.

This initiative is an efficiency and operational refinement effort rather than a strategic exit from any business line or geographic market. The action impacted approximately 2.0% of the Firm's global workforce (~1,600 positions), with the majority of role reductions taking place across the Americas.

---

## 2. Severance Costs & Financial Impact by Business Unit

As accounted for under Compensation and Benefits expense in the Consolidated Statements of Income (and formally disclosed in Footnote 13 of the 2Q 2026 Financial Results), the Firm recognized a total pre-tax severance charge of **$178 million** associated with this action:

| Business Unit Segment | Pre-Tax Severance Expense ($ Millions) | Primary Focus Areas Impacted |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Institutional Securities** | $94.0 | Non-clearing administrative overhead, legacy operations support, and duplicative middle-office trade review roles. |
| **Wealth Management** | $61.0 | Redundant administrative support across branch operations and regional legacy management layers. |
| **Investment Management** | $23.0 | Distribution support consolidation and back-office integration across acquired fund entities. |
| **Total Firmwide Charge** | **$178.0** | Global consolidation across Americas, EMEA, and Asia. |

---

## 3. Headcount Trajectory & Post-Restructuring Metrics

* **Pre-Action Baseline (December 31, 2025):** 84,210 worldwide employees.
* **Q1 2026 Reporting Period (March 31, 2026):** 83,922 worldwide employees (reflecting initial reduction phases in progress).
* **Q2 2026 Reporting Period (June 30, 2026):** 82,944 worldwide employees (reflecting full completion of the March action and selective regional attrition).
* **Target Expense Efficiency Ratio:** The management action forms a key pillar in maintaining the Firm's expense efficiency ratio at **65%** for the first half of 2026, creating structural operating leverage despite elevated market activity.

## 4. Legal, Data Retention, and Post-Employment Obligations

Departing personnel and supervising managers are reminded of binding data security obligations:
1. **Access Revocation:** All system credentials, VPN profiles, and remote cryptographic tokens are revoked effective at close of business on the employee's formal separation date.
2. **Employee Data Retention:** In accordance with the Morgan Stanley Employee & Contractor Privacy Notice (and distinct from retail consumer disclosures under the U.S. Privacy Policy and Notice), personnel records, payroll records, and performance review documentation will be retained in accordance with federal and state statutory timelines (typically 7 years).
3. **Institutional Inquiries:** Former employees or enterprise clients requiring official verification or data rights inquiries regarding internal personnel files must direct correspondence through `dataprotectionoffice@morganstanley.com`.
