# Morgan Stanley Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Results

**Morgan Stanley Reports Net Revenues of \$21.3 Billion, EPS of \$3.46 and ROTCE of 26.6%**

**NEW YORK, July 15, 2026** — Morgan Stanley (NYSE: MS) today reported net revenues of \$21.3 billion for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026 compared with \$16.8 billion a year ago. Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was \$5.6 billion, or \$3.46 per diluted share, compared with \$3.5 billion, or \$2.13 per diluted share, for the same period a year ago.[^1]

> *"Active markets and consistent execution across all three regions drove exceptional results for our Integrated Firm, delivering record revenues of over \$21 billion and record EPS of \$3.46. Excellent results in Institutional Securities were driven by our leading Equities franchise with continued momentum in Investment Banking and Fixed Income. Differentiated content from our Research teams continues to drive high levels of client engagement. Wealth Management added a record \$148 billion in net new assets, with total client assets across Wealth and Investment Management reaching the \$10 trillion milestone. The Integrated Firm is intensifying Morgan Stanley connectivity with clients globally and enhancing financial strength for shareholders. We continue to accrete capital, giving us incremental flexibility to invest in our core businesses while generating strong returns for shareholders."*
> 
> — **Ted Pick**, *Chairman and Chief Executive Officer*

## Quarter Highlights

* **Record Financial Performance:** The Firm reported record net revenues and pre-tax income of \$21.3 billion and \$7.3 billion, respectively.[^12]
* **Strong Profitability Metrics:** Delivered ROTCE of 26.6% [^2],[^6] and ROE of 20.7%.
* **Operating Leverage:** Expense efficiency ratio was 65% for the first half of the year, demonstrating operating leverage alongside business investment.[^3],[^5],[^13]
* **Capital Position:** Standardized Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio stood at 14.8%.[^14]
* **Institutional Securities:** Record net revenues of \$11.0 billion driven by robust client engagement in Equity and continued capital raising/strategic activity momentum in Investment Banking.[^12]
* **Wealth Management:** Record net revenues of \$8.9 billion on higher asset management fees, robust client activity, and higher net interest income, producing a pre-tax margin of 30.5%.[^15] Added \$148 billion in net new assets and \$39 billion in fee-based assets.[^8],[^9]
* **Investment Management:** Net revenues of \$1.6 billion, primarily driven by asset management fees on higher average AUM, alongside positive long-term net flows of \$7.5 billion.[^10],[^11]

## Segment Financial Results

### 1. Institutional Securities

Institutional Securities reported net revenues of \$11.0 billion compared with \$7.6 billion a year ago. Pre-tax income was \$4.3 billion compared with \$2.1 billion a year ago.[^4]

| *Equity underwriting* | \$851 | \$500 |
| *Fixed income underwriting* | \$788 | \$532 |
| Equity | \$6,300 | \$3,721 |
| Fixed Income | \$2,455 | \$2,180 |
| Other | \$(152) | \$202 |

| Compensation | \$2,430 | \$2,980 |
| Non-compensation | \$3,727 | \$2,934 |

#### Revenue & Expense Drivers
* **Investment Banking (up 58%):**
  * *Advisory:* Revenues increased year-over-year on higher completed M&A transactions, particularly in the Americas.
  * *Equity Underwriting:* Increased on higher IPO activity, follow-on offerings, and convertibles.
  * *Fixed Income Underwriting:* Driven primarily by higher client issuances from capital raising and strategic activity.
* **Equity (up 69%):** Record net revenues across businesses and regions, with notable strength in Asia, driven by strong client engagement and favorable market conditions.
* **Fixed Income (up 13%):** Increased due to higher Credit results (credit corporates) and cumulative lending growth in the securitized products business.
* **Other:** Decreased year-over-year primarily due to higher mark-to-market losses on corporate loans, inclusive of hedges.
* **Provision for Credit Losses:** Driven by portfolio growth in corporate loans and secured lending facilities, alongside individual assessments for select corporate and commercial real estate loans.
* **Expenses:** Compensation expense increased due to higher revenues; non-compensation expenses rose primarily due to higher execution-related expenses.

### 2. Wealth Management

Wealth Management reported net revenues of \$8.9 billion compared with \$7.8 billion a year ago. Pre-tax income of \$2.7 billion resulted in a pre-tax margin of 30.5%.[^4],[^15] Net new assets were \$148 billion, of which just over half represented inflows related to client IPOs within the Workplace channel.

#### Revenue & Expense Drivers
* **Net Revenues (up 14%):**
  * *Asset Management:* Increased due to higher asset levels resulting from market appreciation and cumulative fee-based flows.[^8]
  * *Transactional:* Increased when excluding prior-year mark-to-market DCP gains no longer recognized in net revenues.[^16] Driven by higher client activity across products.
  * *Net Interest Income:* Grew primarily on higher average sweep deposits and cumulative lending growth.
* **Total Expenses:**
  * *Compensation:* Rose in tandem with higher compensable revenues.
  * *Non-compensation:* Increased primarily due to higher marketing, business development, and technology investments.

#### Revenue & Expense Drivers
* **Net Revenues (up 6%):** Asset management and related fees increased on higher average AUM driven by market levels and positive flows.[^10] Performance-based income was relatively unchanged, reflecting net mark-to-market gains in private funds.
* **Total Expenses:** Compensation expense declined primarily due to lower compensation associated with carried interest. Non-compensation expenses increased due to higher brokerage, clearing, and technology investments.

* **Share Repurchases:** Repurchased \$1.5 billion (8 million shares at an average price of \$197.64) during 2Q 2026.
* **New Share Repurchase Authorization:** The Board of Directors reauthorized a multi-year common equity share repurchase program of up to \$20 billion, without a set expiration date, effective 3Q 2026.
* **Quarterly Dividend:** Declared a \$1.15 quarterly common dividend per share (an increase of \$0.15), payable August 14, 2026 to shareholders of record on July 31, 2026.

[^15]: Pre-tax margin represents income before provision for income taxes divided by net revenues.
